# Organic Bag Business Proposal

## Project Overview
This entrepreneurship project focuses on establishing a sustainable organic bag manufacturing business aimed at reducing plastic pollution and promoting eco-friendly alternatives.

## Business Concept
The project introduces biodegradable and reusable organic bags made from:
- Cotton
- Hemp
- Bamboo Fibre
- Canvas
- Jute
- Paper Materials

## Mission
To promote environmentally sustainable products while supporting ethical and eco-conscious business practices.

## Vision
- Environmental sustainability
- Ethical manufacturing
- Eco-friendly innovation
- Consumer awareness
